Milemark SMS Privacy Policy

This policy covers the on-demand Milemark race-update SMS program.

Information used

To answer an incoming text, Milemark processes its sender and receiving numbers, message identifier, and message text through Twilio. Milemark stores a keyed hash of the sender/receiving-number pair, the most recently requested race, selection time, and limited message identifiers and counters for duplicate prevention and rate limiting. Milemark does not store the raw phone number or message body in its conversation database. Twilio may retain message logs under its own policies.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to deliver requested race updates, remember your selected race, and protect the service from duplicate requests and abuse. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging service providers, including Twilio and telecommunications carriers, process information as needed to deliver the requested messages; Firebase hosts the service and its private conversation records.

Remembered race and cancellation

A remembered race can be reused for 30 days after selection; expiry prevents reuse and does not mean every record is automatically deleted. Text STOP to cancel and clear the remembered race. Text HELP for assistance. You may continue to use the public race website without texting.
