Race updates by text

Get an on-demand Milemark race update: runner progress, next aid station ETA, and the latest recorded GPS time.

SMS service is awaiting activation. This page describes the opt-in process for the Milemark SMS program.

How to opt in

  1. Copy the race UUID or public viewer URL from the race you want to follow.
  2. Send it by SMS to (855) 760-0810.
  3. After receiving your update, send UPDATE in that conversation whenever you want another update for the same race. Send another race UUID or viewer URL to switch races.

By sending a race UUID, viewer URL, or update request to this number, you consent to receive automated Milemark text-message responses about the race you request at the mobile number you used.

Messages are sent in response to your requests; frequency varies with your requests. There are no marketing messages or scheduled alerts. A response may use multiple SMS segments. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of using the Milemark website.

Text STOP to cancel. Text HELP for help.

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Open messaging app

Opening your messaging app does not opt you in. You must send the message yourself. Viewing this page does not collect consent or enroll you.

What your consent covers

Your request authorizes a reply to your request, not a recurring subscription. Milemark remembers the most recent valid race you requested so you can ask for it again without pasting its ID. Use the public race website without SMS at any time.
