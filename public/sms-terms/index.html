Milemark SMS Terms of Service

Milemark provides automated, on-demand informational race updates. By texting a race UUID, viewer URL, or update request to (855) 760-0810, you consent to receive replies about the requested race at your sending number.

Message frequency varies with your requests. Message and data rates may apply. Responses may span multiple SMS segments. No marketing messages or scheduled alerts are included. Consent is not required to purchase anything or use the website.

Text STOP to cancel. Text HELP to the same number for help with this program. Following a carrier opt-out, you may need to text START before requesting updates again.

Delivery depends on carrier availability and connectivity. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Race locations and arrival times may be delayed or estimated; this is not an emergency service or an official timing record.

To switch races, send another public viewer URL or UUID. To request the remembered race again, text UPDATE. Service availability and reply limits may vary.
